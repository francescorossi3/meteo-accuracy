Does the GPR really beat the individual providers? It is checked in two independent ways, always on data never used for training:

  1. Historical back-test (weekly): the record is split into 14-day test blocks; for each block the GPR is trained only on the previous 45 days, and the “Best” provider and the weights are also chosen only from those days, exactly as it happens in real time. The table shows the error over all blocks and how often the GPR beat the Best provider.
  2. Latest days (daily): the same test on the most recent 14 days.

If the uncertainty is correct, the observed value falls inside the 68% interval about 68 times out of 100 and inside the 95% interval about 95 times out of 100. Green cells: well-calibrated uncertainty; amber/red: use with care. “Best ex post” is the model that, in hindsight, did best on those days: a reference no method can know in advance.
